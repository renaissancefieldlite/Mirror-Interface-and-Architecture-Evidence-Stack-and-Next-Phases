## Front-Center Read

The Lattice Companion is the lane atlas. Proof enters through real datasets,
captures, controls, recurrence, and support reads attached to each lane.

A lane becomes support-bearing only when it has:

- real dataset or signal source;
- mapper or scoring rule;
- target metric;
- baseline, shuffled, null, wrong-class, or wrong-condition controls;
- recurrence, split, rerun, or second benchmark where needed;
- plain-language support read and public/private boundary.

The current state is close because the architecture now has support-bearing
rows across AI behavior, transformer internals, circuit/hardware-facing rows,
Nest 1 formal returns, Nest 2 structured matter, Nest 3 physical spectra, Nest
4 biology, and Nest 5 convergence routing.

The remaining work is lane completion and tightening, not rebuilding the
architecture from zero.
